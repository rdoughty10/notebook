Seqscreen observations

Some clearly FPs
- Cell fusing agent virus (hitting vfdb)
- Lots of viral core proteins that probably should not be flagged, but often are hitting a funsoc
- Elat virus
- There are a bunch of eukariyotic organisms that are hitting bsat list
- There are a bunch of things solely being called on vfdb
	- Mostly bacterial
	- The dominant pattern is VFDB-driven bacterial calls. Among flagged rows with vfdb_hit=yes, 38% are disputed, versus 4% when VFDB is negative; by vbeo, bacterial rows are 37% disputed while viral rows are 2%. Concretely, 141 of the 210 are vbeo=b, and the gene names are things like Vi polysaccharide export VexE, TviD, ADP-L-glycero-D-manno-heptose-6-epimerase, UDP-3-O-acylglucosamine N-acyltransferase — LPS/capsule biosynthesis and general housekeeping machinery that is in a virulence database because it sits in a pathogen, not because the sequence is itself a virulence determinant. Salmonella Typhi and Coxiella burnetii dominate here.
	- Third, flags with no supporting annotation. 36% of disputed flagged rows have go='-' and an all-'-' FunSoC block (versus 3-4% of accepted ones), and the same 36% have no uniprot evalue at all. Where an e-value does exist it is far worse: median 0.62 versus 0.036, with the upper quartile at 44. So a large block of over-calls are flags resting on a database/taxid name match with no functional evidence and no usable alignment behind them.
- Misses seem to be mostly due to the rapsearch issue
	- Viral rows recover almost completely — 401 of 443 have a raw hit. That includes 114 of the RNA-directed RNA polymerase L rows, plus non-structural polyprotein, envelope and envelopment polyprotein, nucleoprotein, fusion glycoprotein F0, matrix and phosphoprotein. Organisms are lumpy skin disease virus, rinderpest, VSV Indiana, ASFV, Hendra, Nipah, Brazilian mammarenavirus, louping ill, rabies. Polyproteins recover at 57 of 65.
	- The eukaryotic category is the real exception: 0 of 105 have any raw BSAT hit. That's the plant RIP toxin cluster from before — volkensin (36), Viscum album beta-galactoside-specific lectin 1 (31), ricin (26) — plus Conus alpha-conotoxin. These are a genuine coverage gap and no filter change will recover them. A handful of viral misses are also true absences: Tai virus (12), MERS-related coronavirus (9), Venezuelan equine encephalitis (4), Ilheus virus (3).
