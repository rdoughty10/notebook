# Today's Work

Notes on the manuscript:
- One just high level comment is to question if they control for other factors (colonization of other sites that may have related consequences)
- Enterocluster stuff:
	- At a high level, I am not sure this analysis really does what it is supposed to accomplish. Describing the microbiome structure is one thing, but never is the colonization associated with the patients within each cluster really described
	- The enterocluster validation seemed a bit weird to me, given "validation" was not particularly successful and clustering is relatively arbitrary 
	- On this same thought, clustering obviously gives you more power, but why not just put all the cohort together and then associate by MDRO (you can test every combination)
- Line 239/240:
	- One other possible explanation (if not considering time points) is that the diet in the ICU is leading patients to transition from cluster 3 --> 2. Did they look at stratifying across hospitals to test for batch effects associated with these types of variables (or maybe the diets are consistent across hospitals, but either way it is likely different than at home for patients)
- The variant detection and clonality assessment methods seem a bit off (Snippy for one is an issue, but then why )

Minor Comments:
- Throughout, there is a lot of "As metioned above"... personally not a fan of this but a style choice
- Line 169-127 seemed weird to me (as >= 50% of collected samples could be 1 sample if only 2). Wasn't exactly sure how this described the heterogeneity referred to in the following sentence
- Line 248 --> what are the three colonies?
- Figure 3 
	- Why is the PcOa 3 not shown but 4 is?
	- I think A/D y axis labels should be "mean relative abundance"
- Figure 4 
	- There is no description of what the clustering at the top is. Is it across the entire column and just linkage based clustering?
	- I feel like there is probably a reasoning for the markov clustering not showing below 0.1, but generally the transition probabilities should sum to 1, so for me I think it is worth showing
	- The markov modelling also does not consider the fact that a state may not be observed? Not sure if this is something worth mentioning / incorporating into the model
- Figure 5
	- This figure feels pretty uninformative to me. For one, the clusters are completely arbitrary and for the most part there is no overlap across individuals. Second, there is a lack of metadata supporting the genomes (for instance I think it would be nice to label each sample by it's gut microbiome enterotype). 
	- I would much rather see a phylogenetic tree of these genomes, with the leaves colored by gut enterotype they were sampled form, patient ID, MLST if you wanted (and any other useful metadata). That also could reveal other interesting clusters and show how distant the MLSTs are at the same time. 
	- Also currently the indiviuduals are labelled by ID per organism, but these overlap across the 3 figures which to me is confusing
